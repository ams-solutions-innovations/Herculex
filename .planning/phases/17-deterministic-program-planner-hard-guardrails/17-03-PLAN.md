<objective>
Guarantee that a `SlotRole.main` slot's exercise stays fixed across every week of a generated block —
matching D-09/D-10's "anchor is a specific exercise, not a movement pattern" requirement — while
still letting a newly-discovered hard-filter exclusion (most commonly injury/pain) break that lock and
force a fresh, safe re-resolution (D-12).

Purpose: today, `RotationAssignments` are built by the week loop applying `pool[epoch %
pool.length]` uniformly to every slot regardless of role — `SlotRole.main` slots on a Concurrent/Block/Max-Effort
periodization model can and do rotate away from the exercise week 1 picked, breaking 1RM/load
progression continuity that strength/powerbuilding users depend on.
Output: a `lockedAnchors` bookkeeping mechanism scoped to one `_createStableSlots` call, intercepting
only the week-indexed `RotationAssignments` loop — never the per-day `used`-set candidate loop this
phase's Wave 1 already extended.
</objective>
