<objective>
Make the five named hard filters (injury/pain, equipment, style, experience, prerequisites) genuinely
absolute in `SmartProgramPlanner`, and replace the current "throw StateError and abort the whole
program" behavior with a per-slot empty result when — and only when — those hard filters legitimately
leave zero candidates. This is the core of PLAN-02 and closes out PLAN-01's "single authoritative entry
point" by deleting the dead `ProgramGenerationRequest` class.

Purpose: today, `_createStableSlots` never leaves a slot empty (comment at line 336), silently
tolerates equipment/eligibility fallbacks it shouldn't, and crashes the entire transaction on genuine
exhaustion. Two of the five hard filters (injury/pain, prerequisites) are not wired into the planner at
all yet — prerequisites verification was built in Phase 16 (`ExerciseProgrammingEligibility.verifyPrerequisites`)
but has zero call sites in `smart_program_planner.dart` today.
Output: injury/pain and prerequisites become real hard filters; the crash-on-exhaustion path becomes a
graceful per-slot `SelectionExplanation.empty(...)` outcome (persisted in Wave 3); `SmartProgramConfiguration`
is confirmed as the sole generation-parameter type.
</objective>
