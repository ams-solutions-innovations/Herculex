<objective>
Close PLAN-04: persist a `SelectionExplanation` for every slot, every week — filled or empty — so the
"why" behind every automatic exercise choice (and every deliberate non-choice) is queryable, not just
implicitly encoded in whether a `ProgramDayExercises` row happens to exist.

Purpose: Wave 1 (17-02) already produces a `SelectionExplanation` for the empty-slot case internally
but discards it (left a `// TODO(17-04):` marker) rather than persisting it, and currently skips
creating a `ProgramExerciseSlots` row for an empty slot entirely — with no `ProgramExerciseSlots` row,
there is nothing for a `ProgramSlotExplanations` row's non-nullable `slotId` foreign key to reference.
Wave 2 (17-03) resolves a `ScoredExercise` for every filled week but never records why it was chosen
beyond the transient `selected.why` string already folded into `ProgramDayExercises.prescriptionWhy`
(a different, method/target-adjustment-focused field — not the slot-selection rationale PLAN-04 needs).
Output: every `_createStableSlots` slot resolution — filled or empty — produces exactly one
`ProgramExerciseSlots` row, and every week of that slot produces exactly one `ProgramSlotExplanations`
row.
</objective>
