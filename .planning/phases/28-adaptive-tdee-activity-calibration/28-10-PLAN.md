<objective>
Reframe the two existing ActivityLevel pickers (D-12, D-13) and give the Profile picker its reset semantics (D-14, D-15).

Purpose: With the estimate now measuring activity, the hand-picked level becomes a seed. The copy must say so, and a calibrated user must be able to nudge it without the app pretending the reset discards their measured history.
Decision recorded here (open item 3): `goals_view.dart`'s `_ActivityLevelSheet` is left unchanged. It writes only `profile.activityLevel`, which is exactly the reseed; the plan 07 controller reacts to that change wherever it is made, so behaviour is correct on all three surfaces even though only two are relabelled (the approved UI-SPEC scopes the copy to Profile and onboarding). Flag as a follow-up for the user if they want the third surface relabelled.
Output: a small pure policy (copy and state rules, unit-tested), and copy/dialog edits in onboarding and Profile.
</objective>
