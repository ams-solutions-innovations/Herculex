D-04 gives a 7-day cadence plus a 7-day grace, so the last fresh observed row is held until it is 14 days old and then falls back. For that hold to start at the first cadence run after logging stops, that run (7 days after the last fresh row) must be able to fail qualification. With a recency of 6 (inclusive: age 0 to 6 passes), a user who logged up to the day of the last run has newest data 7 days old at the next run, fails the gate, and the row is held (aging) for one more cycle before the fallback at 14 days. A user who logs on most days always has the newest data within 6 days.
