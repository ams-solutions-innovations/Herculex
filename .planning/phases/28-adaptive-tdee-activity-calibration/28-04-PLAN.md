    recalibrate (history is newest first; `observedQualified` on rows means gates passed at that cycle):
    - Qualifies now, empty history: NOT promoted (hysteresis, D-03): result is classifier if classification available else coldStart, with observedQualified true.
    - Qualifies now, history.first is classifier/coldStart with observedQualified true (previous cycle also qualified): promoted, method observed, observedQualified true.
    - Qualifies now, history.first is classifier with observedQualified false: NOT promoted, row carries observedQualified true (pending promotion).
    - Qualifies now, history.first is observed (fresh or held): stays observed and fresh immediately (no hysteresis needed for someone already measured, D-15).
    - Does not qualify, history.first is observed, newest fresh observed row is younger than 14 days (cadence 7 + grace 7): HELD (D-04): kcal equals that fresh row's kcal, method observed, observedQualified false, confidence low, windowDays copied, inputs copied plus `held: true` and `measured_at` (ISO of the fresh row's estimatedAt); estimatedAt is asOf.
    - Does not qualify, newest fresh observed row is 14 days old or older: falls back to classifier (or coldStart when classification unavailable), observedQualified false.
    - Does not qualify, no observed rows: classifier when `classification.isAvailable`, kcal = round(bmrKcal * multiplier), confidence from the classification, windowDays = ActivityClassifier.windowDays, inputs = classification.toInputs(); otherwise coldStart with kcal = round(seedMaintenanceKcal), confidence low, windowDays 0, inputs = seedInputs.

    - A result's estimatedAt always equals asOf.

    - Empty history: noEstimate. force true: forced (even the same day).
