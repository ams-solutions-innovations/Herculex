Create `lib/features/weekly_report/domain/iso_week.dart` as plain Dart (no Flutter, no drift, no `DateTime.now()`; every time-dependent method takes the instant as a parameter, per the Clock rule).

Public surface of `class IsoWeek implements Comparable<IsoWeek>`: `const IsoWeek(this.isoYear, this.isoWeek)`; `factory IsoWeek.fromDate(DateTime d)`; `static IsoWeek? tryCreate(int isoYear, int isoWeek)` (null when isoWeek < 1, isoWeek > weeksInYear(isoYear), or isoYear outside 2000..2100); `static int weeksInYear(int isoYear)` (52 or 53, derived from the ISO week of 28 December of that year); getters `DateTime start` (Monday 00:00 local), `DateTime endExclusive` (next Monday 00:00 local), `String startIso` and `String endIso` (Monday and Sunday as yyyy-MM-dd), `IsoWeek previous`; methods `DateTime windowEnd(DateTime now)` (min of now and endExclusive), `bool isAfter(IsoWeek other)`; `static IsoWeek forNotificationTap(DateTime now, String timeHHMM)`; plus `==`, `hashCode`, `compareTo` (year then week), and a `toString` like '2026-W40'.

Implementation rules: derive the ISO year and week with the Thursday rule (the Thursday of the date's week decides the ISO year). Compute the day-difference between the Thursday and 1 January using UTC-normalised day numbers (`DateTime.utc(y, m, d)` difference in days), as `TrendSeries.dayNumber` does, never the difference of two local DateTimes (DST makes inDays off by one). Compute `start` as the Monday of week 1 (the Monday on or before 4 January of the ISO year) plus (isoWeek - 1) * 7 days using `DateTime(y, m, d + n)`; compute `endExclusive` with `DateTime(start.year, start.month, start.day + 7)`. Do not copy `AnalyticsRepository._weekStart` (Duration arithmetic).

`forNotificationTap` per OQ1 (orchestrator default: open the week of the most recent Sunday-at-HH:MM trigger, as RESEARCH recommends; flag in the SUMMARY that the user should confirm this refinement of D-09): parse 'HH:MM' (fall back to 18:00 when malformed or out of range), build the Sunday-at-HH:MM instant on or before `now` (walk back from now's calendar day to the nearest Sunday using the DateTime constructor; if that instant is after `now`, go back 7 days), then return `IsoWeek.fromDate` of that instant. The notification payload is static (a repeating dayOfWeekAndTime notification cannot carry the week), so this function is the only place the target week is decided (D-05, D-09).

Write `test/features/weekly_report/iso_week_test.dart` first (RED) covering every behavior bullet above, including a loop over all 2026 and 2027 weeks for the DST-safety assertions. Do not set the process timezone; assertions must hold in any local zone.
