<objective>
Assemble the pieces into a session: host a buddy session from an active workout, join one from a
scanned token, keep the durable cursor, and tear down cleanly when it ends.

Purpose: BUD-01's join flow, BUD-02's ownership separation at the moment the link is made, BUD-04's
persisted cursor, and BUD-06's safe-leave guarantee. Everything before this plan is a component;
this is where they become a feature.

Output: the QR payload codec, the controller that owns buddy session state, and the Riverpod wiring
plan 11-10 consumes.
</objective>
