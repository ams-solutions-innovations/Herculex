<objective>
Close the split-flexibility gap (D-01-D-03: specialization currently force-resets to Full Body
3x/week/Linear every time it's toggled on, even when the user already has a compatible Upper/Lower
or PPL split selected) and surface the already-shipped, already-lift-agnostic
`PrimaryLiftSpecialization.assistanceFocus` copy in the specialization modal (D-12's UI half - the
domain switch producing distinct assistance slots per sticking point is plan 22-01's job; this
plan makes the user able to SEE that the choice mattered). Also fixes a small but real accuracy bug
(RESEARCH.md Pitfall 3): the summary card's "3 exposures / week" text is hardcoded from when
specialization always forced Full Body 3x - once split flexibility lands here, that number is wrong
for Upper/Lower (2x) or PPL (1x) splits.

Purpose: SPEC-01 requires sticking-point analysis to be visible, not just internally correct;
SPEC-02 requires anchor-lift frequency to reflect the user's real split rather than being silently
overridden.
Output: specialization Apply preserves a compatible existing split; the modal shows lift-specific
assistance copy; the summary card's exposure count is computed, not literal.
</objective>
