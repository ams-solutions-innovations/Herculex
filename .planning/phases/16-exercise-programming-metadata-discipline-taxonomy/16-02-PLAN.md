# Plan 16-02: Curated Asset Ingestion, Companion Mapping & Discipline Taxonomy

<threat_model>
## Threat Model (ASVS L1)
- **Threat T-16-03 (Improper Input Validation / Modality Contamination):** Specialty powerlifting bars (Safety Squat Bar, Swiss bar, cambered bar, trap bar) or non-standard gym apparatus silently assigned to a user selecting `basicWeights`, leading to un-executable sessions, confusion, or equipment unavailability in commercial or home gyms.
  - **Mitigation:** Two-layer hard filtering in `ExerciseProgrammingEligibility.allows` requiring standard gym modalities (`barbell`, `dumbbell`, `cable`, `machine_plate`, `machine_selectorized`, `bodyweight`) AND `programmingCommonness` of `basic` or `common`, explicitly disqualifying specialty bars, chains, boards, and pins without silent fallback.
- **Threat T-16-04 (Safety & Physical Injury / Beginner Escalation):** A novice lifter receiving intermediate or advanced exercises due to uncurated exercises defaulting to permissive values or difficulty levels being relaxed when filling accessory slots.
  - **Mitigation:** Enforce a strict novice difficulty ceiling in `ExerciseProgrammingEligibility.allows` where novice lifters can ONLY receive movements tagged `novice`. Default uncurated legacy rows strictly to `manualOnly` and `advanced`, ensuring that only explicitly curated exercises enter automated generation.
</threat_model>
