<objective>
Ship the user-facing surface: a consent screen that is the only way to turn the feature on, a per-exercise toggle, a live counter during the set, and a review sheet where the user confirms or edits every proposed value. Plus the static test that makes "the tracker cannot write a set" a checked property rather than a convention.

Purpose: REP-03 is the phase's non-negotiable, and this plan is where it is either enforced by layering or quietly lost. The layering *is* the safety property — a guard flag inside a shared service would be one refactor away from failing open, while an import boundary fails loudly at compile and test time.
Output: consent view, per-exercise toggle, tracker panel, review sheet, the widened write-boundary test, and an end-to-end test driving a recorded fixture trace through capture to detection to the sheet.

**Coupling direction (explicit):** `rep_review_sheet.dart` receives an `onConfirm(int reps, int? rpeX10)` callback **injected by `active_workout_view.dart`** and imports **nothing from `lib/features/workouts/` at all** — not `data/`, not `domain/`, not `presentation/`. The sheet does not know `updateSet` exists. `active_workout_view.dart` owns the callback and is the only place it resolves to a write.
</objective>
