<objective>
Wire `CrossfitProgramPlanner` (21-04) and `GppProgramPlanner` (21-05) into `smart_program_planner.dart`'s `_needsFor` dispatch, replacing the bare 2-slot CrossFit stub and the inline GPP `_SlotNeed` literal.
