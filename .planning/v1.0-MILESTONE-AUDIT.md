## 4. Cross-Phase Integration & E2E Flows

All inter-phase wiring contracts were validated across key domains:

1. **Nutrition Flow (Phases 1–6):**
   - Importer (`FoodCatalogueImporter`) caches workbook food data into Drift tables (`foods`, schema v15).
   - Barcode scan (`CAP-01`) directly accesses SQLite lookup with fallback to manual creation.
   - OCR capture (`CAP-02`) feeds nutrition values into editable drafts.
   - Custom meal slots (`DIA-02`) seamlessly persist diary entries (`DIA-01`) and update micronutrient ledger (`NUT-01–03`).
2. **Analytics & Workout Integrity (Phases 9, 11, 12):**
   - `TrainingSnapshot.load` filters out soft-deleted records across all 8 tables (`ANLY-03`).
   - Gym Buddy sessions store a local `buddySessionId` link without mutating partner data or inflating `TrainingSnapshot` tonnage calculations (`BUD-02`, verified by `test/buddy/buddy_analytics_isolation_test.dart`).
   - Non-rep exercises (`EXR-05`, duration, distance, calories) record proper metric units in `set_entries` (Drift v31) but return 0 for `tonnageKg`, preventing volume distortion (`test/set_metric_tonnage_test.dart`).
3. **Ergonomics & Coaching Layer (Phases 12, 13, 14):**
   - Compound exercises in `assets/data/exercises.json` share `movementSlug` tokens (`EXR-01`, `EXR-02`).
   - Ergonomics guidelines in `assets/data/exercise_ergonomics.json` attach to those `movementSlug` identifiers (`ERG-02`).
   - Anthropometry measurements (`ERG-01`) generate signals (`ergonomics.proportion.leg/arm/torso`) fed into `HerculContext` and evaluated by `HerculEngine` (`HRC-01–03`).
   - `ErgonomicsCard` displays biomechanical trade-offs on `exercise_details_view.dart`, hiding automatically when measurements are absent (`ERG-03`).
